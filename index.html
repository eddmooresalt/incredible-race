Patch Notes

Build 63

Maze Finish & Task Control Fixes

6 Aug 2026
  • Added a glowing finish flag at the far end of Neon Maze Chase.
  • Players must collect 22 route dots and then reach the flag; arriving early clearly reports how many dots remain.
  • Moved a compact directional pad into the maze frame so mobile players always have tappable controls, while preserving swipe and keyboard play.
  • Repaired the missing Break the Code Submit Guess button and made its visibility reset whenever the puzzle begins.
Build 62B

Flights, Touchdown & Arcade Upgrade

6 Aug 2026
  • Players can keep choosing nap, chat, or entertainment activities while enough flight time remains.
  • Nap and chat are repeatable; each programme remains unavailable after one watch.
  • Cabin service now arrives once after the first completed activity, then returns players to the activity menu.
  • Every activity consumes its stated flight time, and only shows that fit the remaining journey can be started.
  • Added a cinematic airport departures display with live 24-hour flight times, dynamically generated route cities and flight numbers, plus believable out-of-race destinations.
  • Ride-hail and rental-car arrivals can race for the last early-flight seats; train, bus, and walking arrivals are placed on the later departure.
  • Replaced the touchdown emoji with new cinematic landing artwork that remains in place through the live cash-conversion phase.
  • Added a transition recovery path so touchdown cannot remain permanently stuck over the game.
  • Completely replaced Target Rush with an original 2D neon maze chase featuring route dots, moving patrols, swipe, keyboard and touch controls.
  • Redesigned Mastermind as a polished gradient lock console with clearer feedback keys and attempt history.
  • Fixed a final-sprint ranking bug where the sprint energy deduction could retroactively add up to 27 hidden minutes and drop a leading team to last.
  • Made Fast Forward's 30-minute advantage persist through the final pace and Pit Stop calculations.
Build 62

Fairer Rankings & Mobile Gameplay Polish

6 Aug 2026
  • Rebuilt first-leg standings so rival clocks advance through shared journeys, strong task scores create meaningful advantages, competent teams receive onboarding grace, and a perfect Detour plus perfect Roadblock guarantees first place.
  • Replaced randomized rank previews with performance-based projections while preserving forfeits, Yield penalties, energy consequences, final pacing, and saved progress.
  • Grounded every clue box on a softly blended paved foreground, with dedicated airport tarmac for terminal scenes.
  • Prevented Roadblock performer details and Yield statistics from shrinking or clipping on mobile.
  • Converted partner confidence meters to horizontal blue-to-red scales.
  • Expanded Hit the Beat lanes without changing their 2.7-second timing.
  • Added a clear one-Yield-per-race notice.
Build 61

  • Rebuilt in-flight Chat with the player and partner portraits, alternating speech bubbles, and randomized travel topics over the cabin backdrop.
  • Replaced the low-fidelity Mercado de Coyoacán banner with a sharp, locally hosted 2172 × 724 market artwork.
  • Random race events now appear before racers begin running to the clue box.
  • Replaced the walking emoji animation with cinematic game artwork of two teammates in long travel pants and sneakers.
  • Replaced the broken Bondi Beach banner with sharp, locally hosted artwork.
  • Organized the Admin Playtest Hub into Race Setup, Travel & Flights, Tasks & Challenges, and Results & Checkpoints.
  • Removed the obstructive PERFECT TIMING badge, lengthened the Hit the Beat lanes, and slowed incoming arrows for more reaction time.
  • Preserved all walking costs, time, energy, narration, and progress mechanics.
